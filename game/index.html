NIGHTPLAN: a heist on the architect's blueprints

Plan the job, then pull it: pick one of three entries, buy tools out of your own take, cross rooms under guard and camera cones, crack the target and carry it to the getaway van before a lockdown.

Guards walk patrol routes with red vision cones clipped by walls; awareness fills over time (glimpse, ?, !), so a fair catch needs a guard to hold you in sight and close in. They hear footsteps, sprints, coins and lock-picking, investigate, chase with pathfinding, search, then give up.

Cameras sweep orange cones, fill the alarm meter and radio the two nearest guards. Alarm at 100% starts a 25 second lockdown: reach the van or the police close the building.

Every vault has two ways in: pick the lock under watch or crawl a vent where no one can see you. Green-hatched shadow makes guards notice you four times slower.

Magenta laser tripwires switch on and off on their own clocks, each emitter counting down to its next gap: time your crossing, or EMP the beam. A tripped beam adds 35% alarm and sends guards.

Tools at a cost: rattle coin (noise lure), smoke puck (blocks every cone for 7 s), EMP tag (blinds a camera or laser for 9 s). Every dollar spent comes out of the payout.

Planner's notes (N) draw two live routes from wherever you stand: route A through the vault lock, route B through the vents.

A fair catch: every guard keeps six seconds of what it saw, and the caught page shows the evidence (how long you stood in its cone, when it warned you with ? and !, your distance and the light) on an awareness graph, then points at the shadow or tool that would have saved you.

The getaway sheet draws your whole route on the blueprint with every moment pinned on it (locks, tools, laser gaps, vents, loot, alarms), pays a ghost bonus, a par-time bonus and cash bundles, and checks the plan you made against how the night went. Three floors: The Halvard Gallery, Copperline Savings and the Marrow Tower penthouse.

WASD or arrows move, Shift sprints (loud), hold E to pick, crack or grab, 1-3 picks a tool, click throws it, right-click cycles tools, N toggles planner's notes, P pauses.
