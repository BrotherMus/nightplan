**Nightplan** is a top-down heist game drawn as an architect's blueprint. Pick one of three entries and buy tools out of your own take, then cross rooms under red guard cones and orange camera sweeps, pick a lock or crawl a vent into the vault, crack the target and carry it to the getaway van before the alarm meter hits 100% and a 25-second lockdown seals the building. Two floors: The Halvard Gallery and Copperline Savings & Trust. Plain canvas and ES modules, synthesised sound, no library, no network.

Controls: WASD/arrows move, Shift sprints (loud), hold E to pick, crack or grab, 1-3 pick a tool, click throws it, N toggles the planner's notes, P pauses.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080
```
